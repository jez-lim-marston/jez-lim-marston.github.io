scTEQuant

Bayesian viral & transposable-element quantification

A two-stage Bayesian pipeline for quantifying viral and transposable-element transcription from single-cell and bulk RNA-seq. Developed with AI-assisted tooling and benchmarked head-to-head against established methods, it quantifies locus-specific transcription at transcriptional-unit resolution across bulk, single-cell, and spatial RNA-seq.

Manuscript in preparation — source code is private pending publication.

Stellarscope

Locus-specific transposable element quantification

Locus-specific quantification of transposable element and endogenous retrovirus expression, used to build the first single-cell TE atlas of human cell identity.
